# Lioncrest & Dove website

## Included
- `index.html`: Responsive single-page website with embedded CSS and JavaScript.
- Mobile navigation, practice area cards, contact details, and enquiry form.
- Click-to-call, email, and WhatsApp links.

## Preview locally
Open `index.html` in a browser.

## Before publishing
1. Confirm the firm's official address and office hours.
2. Review all practice areas and service descriptions for accuracy.
3. Confirm that the WhatsApp number is correct.
4. Replace any copy that does not reflect the firm's actual services or claims.
5. Upload `index.html` to your hosting provider's website/public folder, or deploy the folder through your chosen static hosting provider.
6. Configure `lioncrestanddove.com` DNS with your hosting provider's instructions and enable HTTPS.

## Contact form limitation
The contact form uses a `mailto:` link, so it prepares an email in the visitor's email application. It does not send form submissions to a server. To collect enquiries directly on the website, connect a trusted form service or a backend and review privacy/security requirements.

## Important
This is a starter website and does not include legal advice, a client portal, backend, appointment calendar, analytics, or a content management system. Please review all text before publication.
